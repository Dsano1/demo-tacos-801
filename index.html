Our Story

A trompo, a plancha, and a lot of pride.

Tacos 801 started the way a lot of great taco spots do — a family cooking the food they grew up on and sharing it with the neighborhood. Today the trompo turns all day and the plancha never cools down.

We marinate our al pastor overnight, chop carnitas in small batches, and press every tortilla to order.

  • Corn tortillas pressed fresh daily
  • Salsas made in-house every morning
  • Locals' favorite on Salt Lake's west side
